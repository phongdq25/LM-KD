# Scenario 4: Long-CoT Reasoning Chain Distillation

This scenario establishes the common benchmarking protocol for distilling complex, multi-step mathematical reasoning chains and Olympiad problem-solving capabilities from frontier reasoning models (such as DeepSeek-R1) into compact student architectures.

---

## 1. Experimental Models

Evaluated across 3 distinct student models under full fine-tuning and parameter-efficient LoRA regimes:

- **Teacher Model:**
  - `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1` (671B Mixture-of-Experts frontier reasoning model)
- **Student Models:**
  - `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B` (1.5B parameters, evaluated under Full Fine-Tuning)
  - `Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct` (7B parameters, evaluated under LoRA adaptation)
  - `Qwen/Qwen3-8B` (8B parameters, evaluated under LoRA adaptation)

---

## 2. Datasets & Resources

### Training Corpus
- [simplescaling/s1K-1.1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/simplescaling/s1K-1.1): 1,000 highly curated, complex mathematical reasoning traces generated by DeepSeek-R1.
- [GAIR/LIMO](https://huggingface.co/datasets/GAIR/LIMO): Less-Is-More-Olympiad dataset ([`datasets/long_CoT_data/limo/`](../../datasets/long_CoT_data/limo/)).

### Evaluation Benchmarks (Test Sets)
Evaluated across 4 demanding mathematics and competition benchmarks:
1. **AIME 2024:** 30 American Invitational Mathematics Examination problems ([`datasets/long_CoT_data/aime24/test.jsonl`](../../datasets/long_CoT_data/aime24/test.jsonl)) — HF: [HuggingFaceH4/aime_2024](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceH4/aime_2024)
2. **AIME 2025:** 30 recent Olympiad competition problems — HF: [HuggingFaceH4/aime_2025](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceH4/aime_2025)
3. **AMC 12:** High-school competition mathematics ([`datasets/long_CoT_data/amc23/test.jsonl`](../../datasets/long_CoT_data/amc23/test.jsonl)) — HF: [hendrycks/competition_math](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hendrycks/competition_math)
4. **MATH-500:** 500 challenging problems across algebra, geometry, number theory, and calculus ([`datasets/long_CoT_data/math500/test.jsonl`](../../datasets/long_CoT_data/math500/test.jsonl)) — HF: [HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceH4/MATH-500)

## 3. Evaluation Metrics

- **Pass@1 (p@1, %):** Strict accuracy of the first generated chain-of-thought solution.
- **Pass@3 (p@3, %):** Trajectory pass-rate across 3 sampled generations ($T = 0.7$).
- **Verification Engine:** Symbolic extraction of `\boxed{...}` answers verified using `math_verify` equivalence.
- **Aggregated Metric:** Average Pass@1 and Pass@3 across all 4 competition benchmarks.

## 5. Common Generalized Train & Test Scenario

- **Long-Sequence Formatting:** Training sequences (up to 8,192 tokens) contain both thinking tokens (`<think> ... </think>`) and final derivations. Attention-isolated sequence packing is enabled.
- **Credit Assignment & Alignment:** Baselines apply step-aware weighting schedules, segment attribution, or graph spectral alignment over the reasoning graph.
- **Global Token Normalization:** Centralized denominator scaling (`num_items_in_batch`) ensures long mathematical traces do not destabilize gradient accumulation.
- **Testing Phase:** Evaluated under 0-shot chain-of-thought generation with a context limit of 8,192 tokens. Zero-shot baseline results are excluded from fine-tuning rankings.
